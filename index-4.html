💊 Log Tresiba Injection

Each injection is tracked using the pharmacokinetic model:
Onset: +2 hours after injection
Phase 1 (onset → +26h): 75% of dose ÷ 24 = U/hour
Phase 2 (+26h → +38h): 25% of dose ÷ 12 = U/hour
Multiple injections are stacked and summed every 15 minutes.
